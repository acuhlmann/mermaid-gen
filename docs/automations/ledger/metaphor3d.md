## Open observations

- **Closed 2026-08-31, and the diagnosis in it was wrong.** The previous row read "the accented
  item's own name is unreadable in `subway`, and the accent stem is why", and proposed a separate
  accent anchor. Raising the anchor would have fixed none of it: the stem was destroying the name
  by **writing depth** rather than by being drawn on it, and the missing `a` in "Pay" survives with
  the entire callout hidden, because the amber route tube ends at its terminus and rises toward the
  camera. Both are recorded in `docs/agents/domains/metaphor3d.md`; the tube's own half is still
  open for an unaccented terminus (`accent-vs-tube` above). The general lesson: **an anchor guess
  is a hypothesis, and the cheapest test of one is hiding the suspect and shooting again** — one
  screenshot with the marker's `visible` set to false would have refuted this a fortnight ago.
- **A `fillOpacity`-off-live-scene probe drifts across the intro auto-rotate. Kill the rotate
  with `reducedMotion: 'reduce'` on the browser context.** The subway slice's probe read troika
  `fillOpacity` after a 4.5 s settle, and the assumption was that a 1.4 s auto-rotate wave that
  ended three seconds before the sample could not be the noise source. It was: three back-to-back
  runs of the same three composite fixtures at three viewports drifted by up to two labels per
  viewport (toaster phone 5 vs 7 vs 5 across runs). `MetaphorIntro` reads
  `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)').matches` and skips the whole intro when it is
  true, and playwright's `browser.newContext({ reducedMotion: 'reduce' })` is what sets that
  media — one line, and three consecutive runs match to the label. Adding a longer wait was
  measured and did not help (6.5 s and 8 s ran the same drift), because the drift is from the
  declutter pass having stabilised on a not-quite-final camera position, not from opacity easing.
- **The probe in `apps/web/.claude/skills/verify/SKILL.md` still lies, and picking the camera by
  closest `aspect` does not save it.** Tried this run: projecting the accented label's own geometry
  bounding box through that camera put 4 of 12 cases off the top of the canvas, with plausible
  numbers for the rest — the same shape of failure the skill already warns about. What did work,
  and needs no camera at all, is diffing the frame against itself with one object hidden: the
  bounding box of `{ name visible } != { name hidden }` IS the name's screen box, and
  `{ marker visible } != { marker hidden }` inside it is exactly what the marker costs the name.
  Two traps in that technique, both paid for here. A **pixel-count** metric is not comparable
  across a change that alters the label's chip opacity — a more opaque chip swallows the glyphs'
  white outline and halves the measured "ink" while improving legibility, which reads as a
  regression. And **hiding a label lets the declutter pass reveal a neighbour**, which grows the
  diff box until it encloses something else; the accented label being pinned makes this worse, not
  better. Where those bite, count glyphs off the crops — which is what decided this slice, and what
  the previous one did too.
